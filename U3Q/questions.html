1. A researcher wants to estimate the proportion of students at a high school who support a new phone policy. She randomly selects 25 students from each grade level: freshman, sophomore, junior, and senior. Which statements are true?

            II. Grade level is the stratifying variable.
            III. This method guarantees that the sample is unbiased.
            IV. This method may help ensure that all grade levels are represented.
    A. I and II only
    B. I, II, and IV only
    C. II and III only
    D. I, II, III, and IV
