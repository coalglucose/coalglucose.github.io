Unit 3: Collecting Data

General rules:

  1. Read carefully. There is a lot of text in this section.

Topic for this Unit:

Selection bias

  1. Undercoverage bias is when you don’t have a representative sample of the population
  2. Voluntary response bias is when individuals from the community offer their responses.
  3. Non-response bias is when some people don’t respond, skewing results

Response bias

  1. Wording of the question
  2. Appearance and behavior of Interviewer
  3. Respondent lies
  4. Respondent answers
  5. Order of choices

Data collection techniques

  1. Censuses measure the entire population
  2. Samples measure a portion of the population
  1. Simple Random Samples are when each individual and set of individuals is equally likely to be chosen
  1. Names in a hat
  2. Random Digits table
  3. Random Number Generator
  1. Stratified random sample is when the population is divided into subpopulations, and then an SRS is taken of those subpopulations
  2. Cluster sampling is when you select group individuals by similar characteristics and then randomly select entire groups in the sample
  3. Systematic sampling is selecting every kth element in the population
  4. Multi-stage sampling is when you choose the sample in stages growing progressively smaller
  5. Convenience sampling is when you choose individuals that are easiest to reach (datacenter)
  6. Voluntary response sample is when people decide whether to join the sample (online surveys, elections)

Experimental design

  1. Discuss randomization, control, comparison, and replication
  2. Completely randomized design is when you randomly assign treatments with no blocking
  3. Randomized block design is when you split the population according to the categories of some variable
  4. Matched pairs designs is when you block people by themselves/someone heavily similar to them (do the experiment twice at different times, on twins, etc)
