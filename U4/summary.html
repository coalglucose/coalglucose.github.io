General rules:

  • You can write probabilities in fractional form

Basic Probability Rules:

  • Probability
  • Probability = (Desired outcomes) / (Total possible outcomes)
  • Complement Rule: P(Ac)=1−P(A)
  • P(A or B)=P(A)+P(B)−P(A∩B)
  • P(A and B)=P(A)×P(B∣A)
  • Two events are independent if P(A and B)=P(A)×P(B)
  • Mutually exclusive (disjoint) events are events where P(A and B) = 0

Discrete Random Variables

  • When solving, use a let statement
  • The sum of all total probabilities must always equal 1
  • μx​=∑x * P(x)
  • σx2​=∑(x−μ)2 * P(x)

  • Check BINS
