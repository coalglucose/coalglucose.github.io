Unit 5C: 2-Sample Hypothesis Testing

The format of 2-sample HT is the same as 1-sample: you have your hypotheses, assumptions, analysis, and conclusion. There are only slight differences in each section.

HYPOTHESES

There are three types of hypothesis tests we really care about in this unit: dependent sample means, two-sample mean t test and two-sample proportion z test. These tests compare the difference between means and proportions of two samples.

For each of them,

> H0 = the two population parameters will be equal; the difference between the population parameters is 0

> HA = the two population parameters will be different; the difference between the population parameters is not 0 (greater than, less than, or unequal)

ASSUMPTIONS:

You must assume the same three conditions as one-hypothesis testing except for each sample. That is, each sample must satisfy random sampling, independence, and approximate normality of the sampling distribution.

There are also further assumptions for the specific tests conducted:

> Dependent samples: The two samples must be dependent, or paired, in some way

> Two-sample tests: There must be independence between the samples, in addition to being independent within each.
